# 2. Company Positioning

### Primary Positioning

**Engineering-led control systems, OT cybersecurity and plant reliability for critical industrial environments.**

### Company Description

DeepTsight Consulting is a specialist industrial engineering and operational technology consultancy providing engineering support across control systems, E&I engineering, OT cybersecurity, IT/OT architecture, plant reliability and asset lifecycle management.

The consultancy is positioned around practical, engineering-led solutions for heavy industry, resources, utilities and critical infrastructure.

### Core Differentiator

DeepTsight brings together senior hands-on control systems engineering and specialised OT cybersecurity/reliability knowledge.

The approach is intended to ensure that recommendations reflect:

- Actual plant operations
- Physical processes
- Control-system realities
- Safety requirements
- Availability requirements
- Brownfield constraints
- Operational and maintenance realities
- Cybersecurity requirements

Rather than treating cybersecurity, control systems and reliability as isolated disciplines, DeepTsight's positioning connects these areas through an engineering-first approach.
