# slot-home-close: industrial site at dusk, Western Australia

- **Where it appears:** Home page, final section ("Have an operational challenge worth a
  conversation?"), right-hand column beside the enquiry button.
- **Aspect and size:** 4:5 portrait, minimum 1600 × 2000 px. Keep the subject in the middle 70%,
  because the frame crops slightly on small screens.
- **Caption on site:** "Industrial site at dusk, Western Australia."
- **Suggested alt text:** "Process plant structures silhouetted against a dusk sky in Western
  Australia, with the first site lights coming on."

## Intent

The last image before the footer should feel like the end of a shift: quiet, spacious and
unmistakably Western Australian, with no people and no drama. It closes the page calmly and gives
the invitation to talk room to breathe.

## Composition

- Low horizon in the bottom third. The upper two thirds are a clear dusk sky that moves from pale
  warm grey at the horizon to cool blue-grey at the top. Not a colourful sunset.
- Midground: silhouettes of process structures such as pipe racks, a small stack, conveyor
  gantries and a few lattice towers, seen from 500 m or more away so nothing is identifiable.
- A handful of small sodium-orange and white site lights have just switched on. They are tiny
  points, not glare.
- Foreground: flat red-brown Pilbara-style ground or low scrub, slightly out of focus.

**Negative prompt:** the shared negative prompt in README.md, plus: orange sunset, sun disc,
lightning, smoke plumes, burning flares, people, vehicles in focus.

## Photographer's shot list

Shoot from public land only, between 10 and 25 minutes after sunset. Use a tripod at ISO 100–400
and bracket the exposures. Take a vertical frame and a 3:2 horizontal alternate.
