# slot-about-site: site walkdown on an operating process plant

- **Where it appears:** About page, image pair below the career record, left and larger image.
- **Aspect and size:** 3:2 landscape, minimum 2400 × 1600 px.
- **Caption on site:** "Site walkdown on an operating process plant."
- **Suggested alt text:** "An engineer in hard hat and high-visibility workwear walks along a
  pipe rack walkway on an operating process plant in early morning light."

## Intent

This image shows the claim the About page makes: judgement formed on site, not in an office. The
engineer is a small, anonymous figure doing ordinary work. The plant is the subject.

## Composition

- A long perspective along an elevated steel grating walkway beside a pipe rack, with lines
  leading into the frame from the left.
- One engineer, seen from behind or three-quarter behind, about a fifth of the frame height,
  walking away with a clipboard or tablet at their side. The face is not visible. PPE is correct:
  white or blue hard hat, safety glasses, long-sleeve orange or yellow high-visibility shirt or
  coverall with reflective tape, gloves and boots.
- Pipes of several diameters with insulation cladding, valves with handwheels and cable tray
  overhead. Surfaces look clean but used, with no ruined, rusty decay.
- Low early-morning sun from the side gives long, soft shadows across the grating.
