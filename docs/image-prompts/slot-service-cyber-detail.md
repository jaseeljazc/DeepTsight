# slot-service-cyber-detail: zone and conduit drawing under review

- **Where it appears:** Service page "OT cybersecurity and network architecture", part 3.0.
- **Aspect and size:** 3:2 landscape, minimum 2400 × 1600 px.
- **Caption on site:** "Zone and conduit drawing under review."
- **Suggested alt text:** "An engineer's hands marking up a printed network zoning diagram with
  a pen on a site office table."

## Intent

OT security presented as engineering, not hacker theatre. The work happens on paper, with a pen,
next to a hard hat. It is a deliberate contrast to the glowing screens competitors use.

## Composition

- An overhead or high-angle shot of a table. A printed A3 sheet shows an abstract block diagram:
  rectangles grouped inside larger dashed boundaries (zones) and joined by a few lines (conduits).
  There are no real device names or IP addresses, and any lettering is illegible.
- Two hands: one holds a fine black pen and is drawing a dashed boundary, the other steadies the
  sheet. Plain long sleeves in dark navy or grey workwear.
- A hard hat at the top edge of the frame, partly cropped, and a closed laptop with nothing on
  screen.
- Neutral window light.

## Ready-to-paste prompt

> High-angle horizontal photograph of an engineer's hands marking up a printed A3 network zoning
> diagram on a plain table, the diagram shows abstract rectangles grouped inside larger dashed
> boundary boxes connected by a few straight lines, all lettering illegible, one hand drawing a new
> dashed boundary with a fine black pen, the other hand flat on the sheet, dark grey workwear
> sleeves, a white hard hat partly cropped at the top edge, a closed laptop at the side, soft
> neutral window light. Documentary industrial photography, true-to-life colour with slightly muted
> saturation and cool neutral greys, soft contrast, fine realistic grain, 50mm prime at f/4, calm,
> no text, no logos, no glowing screens, no HDR.
