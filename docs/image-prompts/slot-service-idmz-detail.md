# slot-service-idmz-detail: boundary network cabinet in a site switchroom

- **Where it appears:** Service page "IT/OT segregation", part 3.0.
- **Aspect and size:** 3:2 landscape, minimum 2400 × 1600 px.
- **Caption on site:** "Boundary network cabinet in a site switchroom."
- **Suggested alt text:** "A closed grey communications cabinet with a glass door in a clean site
  switchroom, with cable tray running overhead."

## Intent

Segregation is about control and order, so show a closed, locked, tidy cabinet in a switchroom,
not blinking lights. The equipment must not be identifiable (CR-05). The cabinet therefore stays
closed and is seen through glass at a distance.

## Composition

- A medium-wide shot along a switchroom aisle: a row of light-grey (RAL 7035) floor-standing
  enclosures on the left, a painted grey floor and overhead ladder cable tray with neatly laid
  cables.
- The subject cabinet stands in the middle distance with a closed smoked-glass front door. Behind
  the glass there are only indistinct dark rack shapes and a few tiny green status LEDs as soft
  points. No readable labels, logos or port numbers.
- Even, flat cool LED or fluorescent overhead light, as in a real switchroom.
- No people.
