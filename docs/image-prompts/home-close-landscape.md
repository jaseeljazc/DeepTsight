# home-close-landscape: industrial site at dusk, Western Australia (wide)

- **Where it appears:** Home page, the full-width photo band at the top of the closing enquiry
  section ("Talk through an operational challenge").
- **Why it is needed:** the current file `public/images/slot-home-close.jpg` is 896 × 1200 portrait.
  The band is 21:9 and up to about 1200 px wide, so the portrait is cropped hard and upscaled.
  It looks soft on large screens.
- **Aspect and size:** 21:9 landscape, minimum 2520 × 1080 px. On phones the band crops to 4:3
  around the centre, so keep the plant inside the middle 60% of the width.
- **File name when done:** `public/images/home-close.jpg` (tell the developer, who updates
  `img-home-close` in `src/content/source/media.ts`).
- **Caption on site:** "Industrial site at dusk, Western Australia. Representative image."
- **Alt text:** "Processing plant silhouetted at dusk beyond red earth and spinifex"

## Ready-to-paste prompt

> Wide cinematic landscape photograph, 21:9 aspect ratio, of a distant heavy-industry processing
> plant in the Western Australian outback at dusk. The horizon sits in the lower third. Silhouetted
> conveyor gantries, pipe racks, a slim tall stack and process structures stand roughly 600 metres
> away, centred in the frame with open ground either side. A few small warm site lights have just
> switched on. Flat red-brown earth with low spinifex scrub fills the foreground, softly out of
> focus. Calm, clear sky graded from pale warm grey at the horizon to cool blue-grey above, no
> colourful sunset. Quiet end-of-shift atmosphere. Documentary industrial photography, natural
> available light, true-to-life colour with slightly muted saturation, soft contrast, fine realistic
> grain, full-frame camera with a 35mm prime at f/5.6, eye-level vantage, generous negative space.
> No text, no logos, no legible labels, no people, no vehicles, no lens flare, no HDR.

**Negative prompt:** orange sunset, sun disc, dramatic clouds, smoke plumes, burning flares,
lightning, people, vehicles, text, signage, logos, glow, lens flare, HDR, oversaturated colour,
illustration, 3D render.
