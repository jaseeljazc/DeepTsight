## Intent

Reliability as measurement: a real route-based vibration reading taken on real rotating
equipment.

## Composition

- A close to medium shot of a horizontal centrifugal pump and motor on a baseplate. The coupling
  guard is safety yellow, the pump casing is grey or green enamel with honest wear, and a drip tray
  is visible.
- A gloved hand presses a magnetic-base vibration probe against the bearing housing. The analyser
  itself is out of frame or out of focus, with an unreadable screen.
- Background: pipework and a second pump, softly out of focus, in a plant room.
- Natural mixed light from a high window plus overhead lighting.
