# slot-service-control-detail: marshalling cabinet during loop checking

- **Where it appears:** Service page "Control systems and E&I engineering", part 3.0
  (DeepTsight capability).
- **Aspect and size:** 3:2 landscape, minimum 2400 × 1600 px.
- **Caption on site:** "Marshalling cabinet during loop checking."
- **Suggested alt text:** "Open marshalling cabinet with rows of terminal blocks and neatly
  dressed field wiring, a multimeter probe on one terminal."

## Intent

It has to read to an E&I engineer as real work, not stock: neat cable dressing, correct terminal
blocks and a test instrument in use.

## Composition

- A close to medium shot into an open floor-standing enclosure painted light grey (RAL 7035
  style).
- Rows of grey and blue terminal blocks on DIN rail (blue suggests intrinsically safe circuits),
  ferrule-marked wire ends, slotted cable duct with some lids removed and cable ties at an even
  pitch.
- Ferrule markers and terminal numbers are illegible or out of focus.
- A gloved hand enters from the lower right and holds a multimeter probe on a terminal. The meter
  is out of focus and its display cannot be read.
- Even, soft light, as from a cabinet door lamp plus room lighting.
