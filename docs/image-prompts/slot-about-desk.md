# slot-about-desk: drawing markup at a site office

- **Where it appears:** About page, image pair, right-hand portrait image, set lower than the
  left one.
- **Aspect and size:** 4:5 portrait, minimum 1600 × 2000 px.
- **Caption on site:** "Drawing markup at a site office."
- **Suggested alt text:** "Printed engineering drawings with red-pen markups, a scale rule and a
  field notebook on a site office desk."

## Intent

This is the quiet half of the work: checking drawings against what was found in the field. It is
a still life with no people. It should look lived-in and precise.

## Composition

- A top-down or 60-degree view of an A1 or A3 drawing sheet, folded to show part of a title block.
  All text on it is illegible or softly out of focus. Use generic line work only: cable routes,
  single-line symbols and grid lines.
- Red and green pen markups in the drafting redline convention: revision clouds and short
  strike-throughs.
- A triangular scale rule, a mechanical pencil, a well-used hardback field notebook (closed, or
  open at blank grid paper) and a pair of safety glasses resting on one corner.
- A plain grey laminate desk, lit by window light from one side.

## Ready-to-paste prompt

> Vertical still-life photograph from a 60-degree angle of a printed engineering drawing sheet on a
> grey laminate site office desk, generic electrical single-line and cable routing line work with
> all lettering illegible and softly out of focus, red pen revision clouds and green pen checks
> drawn over the linework, a triangular scale rule, a mechanical pencil, a worn hardback field
> notebook with a black elastic band, and clear safety glasses resting at the corner, soft window
> light from the left, shallow but not extreme depth of field. Documentary industrial photography,
> natural light, true-to-life colour with slightly muted saturation and cool neutral greys, soft
> contrast, fine realistic grain, 50mm prime at f/4, calm, no legible text, no logos, no screens,
> no HDR.

**Negative prompt:** the shared negative prompt, plus: readable text on drawings, company names in
the title block, laptop screen, branded coffee cup, hands.
